What did you not tell me?

Surface everything important that you left out, glossed over, or softened:
- Caveats or limitations you didn't mention
- Assumptions you made silently
- Risks you downplayed or omitted
- Alternative approaches you dismissed without saying so
- Things you're not confident about but presented as if you were
- Anything you'd want to know if you were in my position

Be direct. Don't restate what you already said — only tell me what you didn't.
